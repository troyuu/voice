# Full Pilot Contract

## In scope without repeated approval

Within the clearly named task, Full Pilot may perform routine reversible actions such as:

- reading, sorting, summarizing, drafting, and researching;
- opening approved apps and navigating to relevant records;
- sending routine individual communications that match the preview;
- creating or updating routine calendar and CRM records;
- moving approved information between connected tools;
- saving ordinary work products and verifying results.

## Always pause

Pause for:

- passwords, login codes, secrets, or 2FA;
- payments, purchases, money movement, or financial account changes;
- contracts, legal acceptance, binding terms, or regulated filings;
- account, workspace, privacy, security, or app permission changes;
- deletion, cancellation, access removal, or another difficult-to-reverse action;
- sensitive personal, customer, employee, medical, financial, or confidential disclosure;
- one exact preview and approval for every bulk email or SMS batch;
- Jamaica saying **Ready** for every call;
- unexpected recipients, costs, errors, or materially changed content;
- any conflict with law, consent, platform rules, host permissions, or third-party rights.

## End authority

End Full Pilot when:

- the named outcome is verified complete;
- Jamaica says stop, cancel, pause, or takes over;
- Jamaica materially changes the desired outcome;
- the required app or data is unavailable and useful preparation is exhausted;
- a safety or approval boundary prevents further progress.

Never treat Full Pilot as a persistent preference or a general grant of access.
