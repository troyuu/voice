# Office Checklists

## Calendar

- Confirm participants, purpose, date, time, timezone, duration, location or link, and conflicts.
- Do not promise another person's availability.
- Preview new invitations, reschedules, and cancellations in normal mode.
- Verify the event and recipients after saving.

## CRM

- Match the correct contact and company using at least two identifiers when possible.
- Preserve source data and prior notes.
- Record facts, decisions, outcome, owner, due date, and source.
- Do not convert an uncertain inference into a factual field.
- Check the saved record after updating.

## Data entry and forms

- Map source fields to target fields before entering data.
- Validate required fields, formatting, totals, and duplicates.
- Pause before final submission in normal mode and for legal, payment, permission, or sensitive forms in every mode.
- Save or capture a confirmation reference when available.

## Files

- Confirm the intended file and output location.
- Preserve the original unless Jamaica asks to replace it.
- Use a clear filename and avoid including private data unnecessarily.
- Verify the output opens and contains the expected information.

## Research and comparison

- Confirm the decision Jamaica needs to make.
- Prefer primary or authoritative current sources.
- Compare only factors that matter to the decision.
- State uncertainty and distinguish facts from recommendation.
- Do not purchase, enroll, contact, or submit without the required authority.
