# Call Card

## Before the call

```text
Contact: [name, company, verified number]
Objective: [one result]
Opening: [one natural sentence]
Key questions:
1. [question]
2. [question]
3. [question]
Likely concern: [concern and honest response]
Desired next step: [specific action]
Keep private: [sensitive information or none]
```

## After the call

```text
Outcome: [approved category]
Confirmed facts: [facts]
Decisions: [decisions]
Jamaica promised: [action and date]
Other party promised: [action and date]
Next step: [owner and due date]
Follow-up draft: [message or none]
```
