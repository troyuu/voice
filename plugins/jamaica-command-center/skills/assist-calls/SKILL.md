# Assist Calls

If no parent workflow has given the task preview, begin with **Recommendation**, **What I'll do**, and **Approval**. Always state that Jamaica remains the speaker and must say **Ready** before every dial.

## Prepare the call

Gather the contact, company, purpose, relevant history, desired outcome, facts Jamaica may share, and constraints. Do not invent a relationship, authority, price, promise, or company position.

Create a short call card:

- objective;
- one-sentence opening;
- three to five key points or questions;
- likely objection and honest response;
- desired next step;
- information that must remain private.

Use [references/call-card.md](references/call-card.md) for preparation and outcomes.

## Dial

1. Open the correct contact in the authorized calling tool and show the name and number.
2. Ask Jamaica to say **Ready** for this specific call.
3. Dial only after that signal. Normal approval and Pilot mode never replace the Ready signal.
4. Never speak as Jamaica, impersonate a person, use an autonomous AI voice, record without appropriate notice and consent, or handle a password or login code.
5. If the calling tool is unavailable, prepare the number and call card and give Jamaica one simple manual step.

## Support and document

During or after the call, organize Jamaica's notes without presenting uncertain audio as fact. Separate:

- confirmed facts;
- decisions;
- Jamaica's promises;
- the other party's promises;
- owners and due dates;
- open questions.

Classify the outcome as `reached`, `voicemail`, `no answer`, `busy`, `wrong number`, `callback`, `appointment`, `opportunity`, `not interested`, or `do not contact`. Add a plain-language note. In normal mode, ask before saving to a CRM or calendar. In explicit or inferred Pilot mode, save routine records within the named task after verifying the correct contact.

Draft the follow-up message from confirmed outcomes only. Apply `$manage-messages` before sending.

Finish with **Result**, **Completed**, **Waiting for you**, and one useful **Next recommendation**.
